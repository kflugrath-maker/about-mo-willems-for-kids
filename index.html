👋 This is Mo Willems!
Mo is an author and illustrator. He writes the words and creates the pictures for many of his books. He has also worked as an animator, including as a writer and animator on Sesame Street.
✍️

Author

An author writes the words and tells the story.

🎨

Illustrator

An illustrator makes the pictures that help tell the story.

🎬

Animator

An animator helps drawings and characters move on a screen.
